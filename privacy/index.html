2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Rally and the features enabled by your organization.

Account information

Profile information

Depending on the features used by your organization, your profile may include:

  • Date of birth
  • Wedding anniversary
  • Gender
  • Profile photo
  • Other information you choose to provide

Organization and membership information

When you join an organization, we may process:

  • Organization membership
  • Unit and group membership

Attendance information

When attendance is recorded through Rally, we may process:

  • Attendance date and time
  • Attendance status
  • Attendance method
  • Associated organization, unit, group, or event
  • Whether applicable location requirements were satisfied

Location information

Some Rally features use location information. If you use a location-enabled attendance feature, Rally may process your device's location to determine whether applicable attendance requirements have been met.

Rally also supports optional location-based reminders using device geofencing. When this feature is enabled, Rally may use your device's operating-system location capabilities to determine whether you have entered a relevant area and may send an attendance-related reminder.

Geofencing is different from continuous GPS tracking. Rally does not continuously monitor your GPS position.

Location permissions are controlled through your device settings. Disabling location access may prevent certain Rally features from working.

Points and achievements

Rally may maintain records relating to points, achievements, recognition, and related organizational activities.

Announcements

Rally may process information about announcements and whether you have viewed or interacted with them.

Device and technical information

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system
  • Rally application version
  • Push-notification information
  • Technical logs
  • Diagnostic information
  • Crash information
